How it works

From “this matters” to a complete note

Capture the moment and its context without breaking your viewing rhythm.

01

Open the side panel

Open a YouTube or Bilibili video, then select Video Notes in the Edge toolbar.

02

Add your thought

Selecting the editor pauses the video. Move focus away to save the note and resume playback.

03

Keep the context

Attach a player screenshot, recent subtitles, or a hold-to-talk voice recording.

04

Export and refine

Export a Markdown ZIP and continue editing in the notes app you already use.

Features

Every note keeps the context around it

⌁

Automatic lead-in subtitles

Choose 5, 10, 20, or 30 seconds. Capture native subtitles or bilingual subtitles shown by Immersive Translate, then edit each subtitle block when needed.

Local-first

Your study record stays on your device.

Notes, subtitles, screenshots, recordings, and transcriptions live in extension storage. Model weights come from a pinned source and are checked for exact size and SHA-256 before being cached.
